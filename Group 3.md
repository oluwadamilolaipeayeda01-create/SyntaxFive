PRD: Campus Delivery Service

Status: Draft v0.2 
Date: November 25, 2026 
Source: Campus Transportation Survey

1. Problem

Students must wear corporate attire before 5:00pm. On lecture-free days many skip meals or delay package pickups rather than dress up for a short errand. The cafeteria's ID check-in also stops friends from collecting meals for them.

2. Goal

A campus delivery service that brings food and packages (including parcels from the campus drop-off point) to students' hostels, within school rules.

3. Users

Students (primary): hostel residents who want food and packages without wearing corporate.
Dispatchers: verified students, approved staff, or vetted riders.
Stakeholders:university administration and cafeteria management.

4. Key Requirements

 Area  Requirement  Priority

 Ordering : Request food, package, or errand delivery with hostel and room details | P0 |
 Pricing : Show fee before confirming (₦200 to ₦1,000, higher for heavy packages) | P0 |
 Dispatchers : Verify every dispatcher; show their identity on each order | P0 |
 Cafeteria : Support direct digital ID/meal plan verification, or dispatcher takeout paid by transfer or cash | P0 |
 Payments : Bank transfer and cash on delivery | P0 |
 Channel : Launch on the channel students prefer (app, WhatsApp, or SMS) | P0 |
 Tracking : Status updates from accepted to delivered | P1 |
 Feedback: Ratings and issue reporting | P2 |
Subscription : Weekly or monthly plan | P2 |

6. Trust and Compliance

- Verify all dispatchers before activation.
- Agree hostel access rules with the university.
- Respect cafeteria ID rules; do not bypass them.
- Collect only the personal data needed.

7. Success Metrics

Weekly active students, orders per week, average delivery time, on-time rate, repeat usage, and average rating. Targets to be set after survey analysis.

8. Release Plan

1. Validate: analyze survey, meet administration and cafeteria.
2. Pilot: one or two hostels, small dispatcher pool, non-cafeteria food and packages.
3.  Expand: more hostels, cafeteria flow if approved, tracking and ratings.

9. Risks

Risk: Mitigation
 Cafeteria integration not approved | Fall back to non-cafeteria food and dispatcher takeout |
 Hostel access restricted | Use verified student dispatchers and agreed drop-off points |
 Too few dispatchers at peak times | Incentives and a small pilot scope |
 Safety or trust concerns | Verification, named dispatchers, ratings |
 Price too high | Tiered pricing validated against Q9 |
